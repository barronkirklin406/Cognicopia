Frequently Asked Questions

Activities built from a resident's own life

Common questions from family caregivers, clinicians, and facility directors about using Cognicopia.

What makes Cognicopia different from standard coloring books?

Standard coloring and activity books are frequently designed for children or casual hobbyists. Cognicopia creates adult-centric, evidence-based reminiscence workbooks utilizing high-contrast lines, clean white spaces, and era-specific historical prompts that honor a senior's life experience.

How does Cognicopia help reduce caregiver stress?

Planning daily activities for someone with cognitive decline can lead to burnout. Cognicopia removes the guesswork by instantly generating structured, ready-to-use worksheets and prompts that require zero preparation time.

Can these workbooks be used in professional memory care facilities?

Yes. The platform is built to support both individual family caregivers and professional memory care coordinators, speech therapists, and assisted living activity directors looking for standardized engagement tools.
